# 🎹 NooB Piano — The Super Fun Piano Adventure! 🐰✨

Welcome to **NooB Piano**, the magical music game that turns learning the piano into a colorful, exciting quest! 🌟

Whether you've never touched a piano before or you want to play your favorite cartoon and video game tunes, NooB Piano makes every note feel like winning a game!

### 🌈 1. Rainbow Falling Notes
Forget boring sheet music! Watch colorful music notes fall down your screen straight onto the piano keys. Just press the matching key when the note reaches the bottom line!

### 🐰 2. Maestro Bunny — Your Musical Best Friend!
You are never practicing alone! Maestro Bunny dances and cheers for you with confetti when you get high scores, and gives you sweet hugs and cheerful smiles to keep going when things get tricky.

### 💎 3. Collect Shiny Medals & Level Up!
Start at Level 1 and climb your way to Grand Maestro! 
- Earn **🥉 Bronze, 🥈 Silver, 🥇 Gold, and 💎 Diamond Medals** as you master each song.
- Explore **60 fun songs** made just for kids — starting from easy 1-finger nursery rhymes all the way to exciting classical showpieces!

### 🐢 4. Magic Slow-Motion Practice
Learning a fast song? No problem! Use the **Turtle** 🐢 or **Snail** 🐌 slow-motion button to slow the song down so your fingers have plenty of time to learn each move. Speed it back up to **Turbo** 🔥 once you're a pro!

### 🎹 5. Works With Real Pianos, Keyboards, or Your Screen!
- **Real Piano or Keyboard in your room?** The game hears your piano through your computer microphone!
- **USB Keyboard?** Plug it in and play with zero delay!
- **No piano at home yet?** Play directly with your mouse or keyboard right on your computer screen!

### 🎬 6. Play Your Favorite YouTube Songs!
Love songs from Disney, Super Mario, Minecraft, or anime? Search for any piano video on YouTube inside the app, and NooB Piano will load it so you can play along!

### 🏆 7. Worldwide Hall of Fame Leaderboard!
Show the world your skills! Every song you finish earns points toward the **Global Leaderboard**. Can you make it to the Top 3 and wear the Golden Crown? 👑

## 🚀 How to Start Playing in 3 Easy Steps!

1. **📥 Download & Open NooB Piano** on your computer.
2. **📚 Open the Song Academy** and pick your favorite starter song (like *Twinkle Twinkle Little Star* or *Baby Shark*).
3. **🎹 Follow the falling notes**, hit the beat, and celebrate your first Gold Medal with Maestro Bunny! 🎉

---

## 🎯 Game Modes for Every Pianist

- **🧘 Practice Mode (Wait Mode):** The song stops and waits for you to find the right key! Perfect for your very first try.
- **⚡ Arcade Mode (Rhythm Mode):** The song flows with the beat! Test your reflexes, build huge combos, and rack up high scores!
- **🔍 Full View & 🎯 Zoom View:** Zoom in on just the notes you need, or see all 61/88 keys like a concert stage!

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Note for Parents & Music Teachers
NooB Piano is designed to spark a lifelong love for music through interactive play, positive encouragement, and instant visual feedback. It safely trains ear recognition, hand coordination, and musical rhythm in a joyful, stress-free environment.
